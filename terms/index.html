Lastara uses exchange rates from a third-party provider, normally refreshed daily, to help you plan and track your travel budget across different currencies.

Each expense or income entry keeps the exchange rate used when it was recorded, so past entries remain consistent as rates change. If you change an entry's currency, it is converted at the current rate.

Actual exchange rates may vary depending on your bank, card provider or currency exchange service, including any fees they charge. Converted amounts are estimates for budgeting and forecasting rather than guaranteed transaction rates. Currency conversion may be temporarily unavailable if exchange-rate data cannot be retrieved.
