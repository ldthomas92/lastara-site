The problem

Knowing what you’ve spent isn’t the same as knowing it will last.

Most travellers know how much money they have. Fewer know whether it will stretch across every country they plan to visit — until a few weeks in, when it’s too late to plan differently.

Many expense apps look backwards. Lastara looks ahead: it ties your budget to the route you actually intend to take.

Who it’s for

Anyone travelling long enough for the budget to matter.

  • Solo travellers and backpackers
  • Gap-year travellers and travelling couples
  • Digital nomads and slow travellers
  • Families travelling long-term

Why we built it

Built for the journey we’re living.

Lastara started with a question while planning our family’s long-term travels: would our money last across all the places we wanted to visit?

We wanted something that looked beyond today’s spending and helped us understand what our budget meant for the journey ahead. So we built Lastara — and now we’re using it as we travel.
