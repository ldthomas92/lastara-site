Privacy Notice

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information the Lastara app collects, why, and what happens to it. 2Lastara in brief

Lastara helps long-term travellers plan a route, set a budget for each destination, log their spending and income as they go, and see how their spending affects the journey ahead. This notice covers the Lastara mobile app on iOS and Android, including its optional receipt-scanning feature. It also covers the beta application form linked from lastara.co.uk (Section 3.6). It does not cover any other product or any website you reach by following a link out of the app.

3Information we collect

3.1 Account and sign-in information

Lastara does not have its own password system. You sign in with your existing Google or Apple account, and we receive a unique identifier for your account and, depending on the provider and what you choose to share, your email address. We don't ask Apple for your name. Depending on the provider, the sign-in record kept for your account can also include your name and a link to your profile picture (Google provides these); Lastara doesn't use or display either.

3.2 Journey, budget and spending information you provide

This is the core of what Lastara stores on your behalf: your journey name, your overall journey budget and its currency, the destinations and date ranges you plan, a budget for each destination, and every expense and income entry you log — amount, currency, category or income source, date, an optional note, and which destination it belongs to. This is only ever shown to you directly in the app, subject to Section 7 below.

3.3 Receipt photos

You can photograph a receipt and have its amount, currency, category, date and a short description filled in automatically. If you use this, the photo is sent from your device, over an encrypted connection, to a Lastara function hosted by Google Firebase in Singapore, which passes it to Google's Gemini AI service for that one-off reading and returns the result to the app. Lastara uses Google's paid Gemini service. Under Google's terms for the paid service, content sent to the service is not used to improve Google's products or train its models, and Google may keep it for a limited period for abuse detection and legal compliance. Lastara does not keep a copy of the photo in its database or storage. To limit use of the feature, we record, for each account, the date and how many receipts it has scanned that day (currently up to two a day); this record contains nothing from the receipt itself. Like any camera feature, the app saves the photo temporarily in your phone's own storage while you scan. Lastara does not delete that copy itself; it stays until your phone clears it in the ordinary way it manages app files.

3.4 Information collected automatically

If you choose to allow it, Lastara uses a product analytics service (PostHog, hosted in the EU) to understand how the app is used and to find problems. We ask you once, the first time you reach your journey, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings under “Share usage data”. If you say no, or switch it off, nothing is collected or sent, and Lastara works exactly the same.

If you allow it, Lastara records when the app is installed, updated, opened, and moved to and from the background, and a small number of actions: creating a new journey (with its base currency only), logging an expense (with its category, whether it is spread over several days and, if so, the number of days), logging an income entry (with its income source), scanning a receipt (with its category only), and turning on spreading an expense over several days. Each event also carries basic technical information: app name and version, device type, screen size, language, time zone and a session identifier. We never send your spending amounts, notes, receipt contents, destinations or journey names, and analytics is not linked to your account, name or email address.

Events are linked to a random identifier created on your device when you allow analytics. It isn't your name or email address, but it stays the same across sessions, so we treat it as personal information. If you switch analytics off, the identifier and any events not yet sent are deleted from your device straight away, and nothing further is sent. A transmission that was already under way at that moment may still complete, and events already received by PostHog are not removed by switching analytics off (see Section 9). We ask PostHog not to work out your location from your IP address. Like any internet service, PostHog's servers see your device's IP address when your device connects; PostHog is set to discard IP addresses rather than store them. See Section 9 for how long events are kept. We do not use analytics to build advertising profiles.

3.5 Information we do not collect

Lastara does not request your location or contacts, and does not record audio. (The app's technical permissions list includes the microphone because of how its camera component is packaged; Lastara never uses it.) Receipt scanning takes a photo with your camera; Lastara does not browse your photo library. We do not show advertising and do not collect an advertising identifier.

3.6 Beta applications on this website

If you apply to test Lastara through the form linked from lastara.co.uk, you are taken to a form hosted by Tally (tally.so), which has its own privacy policy. The form asks for your name, your email address, which kind of traveller you are, where you are in your travel journey, and whether you use an iPhone or an Android phone. You must tick a box to confirm you have read this notice and understand that we will use your details to contact you about beta testing. We use these answers only to choose beta testers and to contact you about testing Lastara. Applying does not guarantee a place, and you do not have to agree to anything else to apply.

The form also has a separate, optional box, which is not ticked unless you tick it, asking whether you would like one email when Lastara launches publicly. Tick it only if you want that email. If you do, we use your email address for that purpose with your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time by emailing privacy@lastara.co.uk. Without that tick, we do not use your details for marketing. The box does not affect whether you are selected to test.

Tally stores your answers on our behalf and sends us an email notification when you apply. If we invite you, we may share your email address with Apple (TestFlight) or Google so that they can deliver the invitation.

We keep applications only for the periods set out in Section 9. You can ask us to delete yours at any time at privacy@lastara.co.uk; we normally respond within one calendar month.

This website does not use analytics, advertising cookies or other trackers, and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. Like any website host, the service that hosts it (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address and basic request details when you load a page. When you follow the link to the form you leave this website and Tally's own policy applies.

4How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and recognise you when you sign back in.
  • To store your journey, destinations, budgets, expenses and income, and to calculate your spending forecast.
  • To convert amounts between currencies, using daily rates from Open Exchange Rates.
  • To read a receipt photo you choose to scan, using Google's Gemini service.
  • If you allow it, to understand how the app is used and to find and fix problems, using PostHog analytics.
  • To choose beta testers and contact people who apply to test Lastara, using the website application form.
  • If you ticked the optional box on that form, to email you once when Lastara launches publicly.
  • To communicate with you about your account where necessary — for example a critical security notice.
  • Possibly, in future, to build aggregated and genuinely anonymised statistics about travel spending — see Section 7.

We do not use your spending or journey information to build an advertising profile, and we do not let any third party use it to target advertising or marketing at you individually.

6Who we share information with

Lastara uses a small number of third-party services.

  • Firebase (Google): stores your account and journey data on our instructions — your account identifier, email, and everything in Section 3.2 passes through this service.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: when you sign in, you interact directly with Google's or Apple's own sign-in system. We never see or store your password; we only receive the identifier (and email, if shared) needed to recognise your account afterward.
  • Google Gemini: reads a receipt photo you choose to scan, for that one request, as described in Section 3.3. The photo reaches Gemini through a Lastara function hosted on Firebase (Google).
  • Tally: hosts the beta application form and stores the answers you submit, on our behalf, as described in Section 3.6. Tally is a Belgian company acting as our processor under its data processing agreement. Its email-delivery provider (in the United States) handles the notification emails it sends us.
  • Apple (TestFlight) and Google: if we invite you to test, your email address may be passed to Apple or Google so they can deliver the invitation.
  • Open Exchange Rates: your device requests the day's conversion rates directly — the same request for every user, containing nothing about you or your journey. As with any internet request, the provider sees your device's IP address.
  • PostHog: if you allow analytics, processes the usage information described in Section 3.4 on our behalf under a data processing agreement we have signed with it, on servers in Germany (PostHog's published list of its own sub-processors is at posthog.com/subprocessors). It receives nothing if you have not allowed it.

Beyond this, we only share personal information where required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Lastara, our users, or others.

7Possible future use: aggregated travel intelligence

We may use journey and spending information from across Lastara to build aggregated, anonymised travel-cost intelligence — for example, typical budgets for a destination, or how spending varies by season. This isn't something Lastara does today. In future, it could help us improve Lastara's own forecasting, and could support research, benchmarks, or commercial insights we provide to other organisations interested in travel spending.

We do not intend to sell any individual user's identifiable journey or spending history, and we do not intend to let any third party build a profile of an individual Lastara user. Any information we share externally would be aggregated and anonymised, designed so that no individual traveller could be identified from it. If we pursue this, we'll complete the legal groundwork it requires — including confirming the lawful basis and whether additional consent or an opt-out is needed — before it becomes an active feature, and we'll update this notice accordingly.

8International data transfers

Because our service providers operate internationally, some personal information is processed outside the UK. Your account and journey data is stored in Google's Firestore database in Singapore, and the receipt-reading function also runs in Singapore. Google's terms for the Gemini service say content sent to it may be stored transiently or cached in any country where Google or its agents have facilities. PostHog's EU instance stores analytics data on Amazon Web Services servers in Germany, although PostHog uses a worldwide content-delivery network (Cloudflare) to route traffic to them; for the other services in Section 6, data may be processed in the United States or other countries, under each provider's standard safeguards for international transfers. Tally says it stores form responses in the European Union, which the UK treats as providing adequate protection. Its agreement with us allows transfers outside the EEA only under approved safeguards, and some of its own providers, such as the one that delivers email notifications, are in the United States.

9How long we keep information

We keep your account and journey information for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (see Section 11), we delete your journey, destination, expense and income data from our active database straight away; it may briefly persist in Google's standard infrastructure backups until those expire, which is outside our direct control. Analytics events, if you allow them, are kept by PostHog for the period set by our PostHog plan, which is at most seven years (one year on PostHog's free plan, seven years on its paid plans); PostHog does not allow us to shorten that period. If you withdraw your consent, the identifier on your device is deleted and no further events are sent. Withdrawing consent stops future collection; it does not delete or anonymise events already sent to PostHog, which stay there until the retention period above ends.

Beta applications. If you apply to test Lastara and are not selected, we delete your application within 90 days of the date you registered. If you are selected, we delete your registration details within 90 days after the beta ends, unless we are legally required to keep them longer. We delete them from Tally, from Tally's trash, and from the notification emails we received; Tally removes deleted data from its own backups within 90 days. You can ask us to delete your application sooner at privacy@lastara.co.uk, and we normally do so within one calendar month.

Launch announcement list. If you ticked the optional launch box, we keep your email address for that purpose only until we have sent the launch announcement, you withdraw your consent, or 12 months after you signed up, whichever comes first. We keep it separately from the beta selection described above.

10Your rights and choices

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • object to uses based on our legitimate interests (including beta applications);
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it: for analytics, switch off “Share usage data” in Settings; for the optional launch email, email privacy@lastara.co.uk; and
  • complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you think we've mishandled your information.

You can already do much of this yourself: Settings shows the email address tied to your account, and the app can export your journey, destination, expense and income data as a CSV file at any time. Switching analytics off stops future collection (see Sections 3.4 and 9). For anything else, contact us — see Section 15.

11Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account from Settings at any time, without contacting us. For your security, we'll ask you to confirm you're still signed in first. Once confirmed, we delete every expense, income entry and destination in your journey, then your journey record, then your sign-in account — each step runs immediately, one after the other. This removes everything we hold in your account and journey data, subject to the backup note in Section 9. If you had allowed analytics, deleting your account also switches it off and deletes the analytics identifier on your device, and Lastara will ask you again if you use it on this device later. Analytics events already recorded are not linked to your account or name, and once the identifier is deleted we cannot tell which events were yours, so they are not removed individually; they are deleted when they reach the end of the retention period in Section 9. The small daily receipt-scan counter described in Section 3.3 (a date and a number tied to your account identifier) is not currently removed in this process. This cannot be undone.

12Minimum age

Lastara is intended for adults aged 18 and over planning their own travel budgets. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

13Security

Your journey and spending data is stored in Google's Firestore database (in Singapore), configured so that it should only be accessible to your own signed-in account. We don't keep your receipt photos. We use Google's and Apple's own secure sign-in rather than storing passwords ourselves.

14Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time, for example as the app changes or the law requires. We'll update the date at the top when we do, and let you know in the app if a change is significant.
